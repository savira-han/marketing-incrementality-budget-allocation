# Business Context

## 01 - Business Setting

The company is an online travel platform (OTA) focused on hotel bookings.

Customers can discover hotels, compare options, and complete bookings through the platform. Marketing is used to generate demand across multiple channels, including Google, Meta, TikTok, and CRM.

The business operates across multiple geographic areas, with differences in customer demand, purchasing behavior, marketing intensity, and seasonality.

## 02 - Marketing Environment

The company currently invests approximately **$10M per year** across:

| Channel | Role                                     |
| ------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Google  | Paid acquisition and demand capture      |
| Meta    | Paid acquisition and demand generation   |
| TikTok  | Paid acquisition and demand generation   |
| CRM     | Customer re-engagement and retention     |
| Organic | Non-paid demand and measurement baseline |

The current paid-channel allocation is approximately:

* Google: 40%
* Meta: 30%
* TikTok: 20%
* CRM: 10%

This 4:3:2:1 allocation represents the **starting condition**, not the target allocation.

Organic activity is treated as a measurement baseline and is not eligible for the additional budget.

## 03 - Business Problem

The company is planning for the next quarter and has an additional **$2M marketing budget** available.

Current reporting can describe how much revenue and contribution margin are associated with each channel, but observed performance does not necessarily represent the business impact caused by marketing.

A customer may have purchased without seeing an advertisement, while customers who interact with several channels may be counted multiple times by platform-level attribution.

This creates an important distinction between:

* **Attributed performance** - outcomes assigned to marketing activity
* **Incremental performance** - outcomes caused by additional marketing investment

The business therefore needs to understand not only where conversions are attributed, but where additional marketing spend creates additional contribution margin.

## 04 - Decision to Be Made

The primary business question is:

> **Given an additional $2M marketing budget for the next quarter, how should the budget be allocated across Google, Meta, TikTok, and CRM to maximize expected incremental contribution margin?**

The analysis is intended to inform a budget decision rather than simply rank channels by historical performance.

The decision requires understanding:

* How much business is incremental to marketing
* How attribution distributes credit across customer journeys
* How marketing investment affects purchases and revenue
* How revenue translates into contribution margin
* How channel returns change as spend increases
* Where the next dollar of marketing spend is expected to generate the most incremental contribution margin

## 05 - Measurement Challenge

Several forms of measurement are required because no single metric answers the business question.

### Attribution

Multi-touch attribution is used to understand how marketing interactions contribute to observed purchases.

Attribution helps describe the customer journey, but it is not treated as causal evidence.

### Incrementality

A geographic experiment is used to estimate the causal impact of increasing total marketing investment.

Twenty areas are assigned to treatment or control using stratified randomization.

Treatment areas receive a 25% increase in total marketing spend while control areas remain at baseline.

The experiment estimates the aggregate incremental effect of increasing marketing investment. It does not independently identify the causal effect of Google, Meta, TikTok, or CRM.

### Economics

The primary economic outcome is **contribution margin**:

`Contribution Margin = Revenue - Subsidy - Discount`

Marketing spend is kept separate from transaction-level variable costs.

This allows marketing efficiency to be evaluated against the economic value generated by additional demand rather than revenue alone.

## 06 - Analytical Scope

The project connects four analytical layers:

**Funnel → Attribution → Incrementality → Economics**

These layers are then used to estimate channel response and marginal economics before evaluating how the additional $2M could be allocated.

The analysis will cover:

* Marketing performance
* Customer marketing journeys
* Multi-touch attribution
* Geographic incrementality testing
* Customer economics
* Channel response to spend
* Marginal contribution economics
* Budget allocation scenarios

The project uses synthetic data designed to represent a realistic OTA marketing environment.

The synthetic dataset is constructed as one coherent business process rather than as independent datasets. Hidden simulation truth is maintained separately so that analytical estimates can be evaluated against known underlying effects.

The final budget allocation is an analytical output of the project, not a predetermined answer.
